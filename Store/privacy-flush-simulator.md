# Flush Simulator — Privacy

*Suggested location: https://c6ellc.com/privacy/flush-simulator/*

Deliberately short, and deliberately specific to this one app. The general C6
policy describes practices — logs, accounts, payments, crash reporting — that
apply to other products but not to this one, and linking it as the App Store
privacy policy would contradict the "No data collected" label on the listing.
The general policy already allows for this: a Product may publish its own
notice, and that notice governs.

---

## Flush Simulator

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

Flush Simulator collects nothing.

There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no
third-party SDK of any kind in the app. It makes no network request of its own.

### What stays on your device

Your progress — lifetime flushes, best run, best streak, which fixture you have
equipped, your daily result, and whether you muted the sound — is stored on your
device using Apple's standard app storage. It never leaves the phone, it is not
readable by us, and deleting the app deletes it.

### Game Center

If you open the global leaderboard, your score is submitted through Apple's Game
Center. That is Apple's service, not ours: the nickname shown is the one you set
with Apple, and what Apple does with it is covered by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We receive no
personal information from it and cannot identify you from a leaderboard entry.
If you never open the global tab, nothing is ever submitted.

### Sharing a daily result

The daily challenge offers a share button. That hands a short block of text to
the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is transmitted to us.

### Children

The app is rated for general audiences, has no chat, no user-generated content,
and no way for players to contact one another. Because nothing is collected,
nothing is collected from children either.

### Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will say so
before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to
match.
